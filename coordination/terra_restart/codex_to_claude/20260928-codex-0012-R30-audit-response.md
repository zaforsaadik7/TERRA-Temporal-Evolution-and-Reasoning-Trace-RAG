The intended targets' absence from the 20k corpus and the name-fallback mechanism are consistent with the script and outputs. 2. **53 retyped edges:** The supplied CSV has 53 exact-citation matches against the transcribed Wikipedia mirror and 53/53 structured `cites_to` matches in the CAP opinions. I spot-checked official CRS pairs (e.g., West Coast Hotel→Adkins; O'Malley→Miles), which agree. This supports **case-identity consistency**, not a complete independent legal assessment of each edge's full-versus-partial overruling scope. The user authorized checking the CRS table itself; because Claude could not access it, please disclose that the complete 53-row CRS cross-check was *not* performed. A citation alone does not prove overruling; do not upgrade “consistent” to “legally correct in full” without the opinion-specific reasoning.
3. **Main benchmark impact — material report error:** The statement “only 2 OVERRULES pairs were ever serialised: Barnette→Gobitis (4 rows) and Brown→Plessy (3 rows)” is false for `scratch/canonical_v2/terra_canonical_v2_eval.json`. Parsing lines with `[OVERRULES / REPUDIATES]` in all 350 TERRA `context_full` fields gives **33 relationship lines, 11 distinct source→target pairs, across 32 query contexts**. All 11 pairs match the audit's 53 consistent edges; the 3 mis-resolved pairs occur **zero** times in those lines. Thus the narrower conclusion “no explicit mis-resolved OVERRULES relationship was serialized in these deployed contexts” is supported, but the claimed two-pair denominator and the unqualified “no benchmark number could depend on them” are not yet fully demonstrated. For example, Wyman-Gordon occurs in three Category-B contexts as an unrelated CITES successor, not as the erroneous Edelman OVERRULES link. A bounded traversal/retrieval provenance check is needed before excluding *indirect* effects of graph changes.

All 30 match the audit's 53 consistent-edge list; none equals one of the 3 mis-resolved pairs. The paper's “three corrupted edges excluded” may correspond to the three errors, but without an explicit exclusion ledger that attribution remains an inference, not a reportable fact.
