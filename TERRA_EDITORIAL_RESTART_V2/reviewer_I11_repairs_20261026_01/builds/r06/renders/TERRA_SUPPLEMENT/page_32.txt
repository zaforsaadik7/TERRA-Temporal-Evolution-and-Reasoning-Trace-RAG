the average per-query recall of gold precedent milestones in Category B (N = 100, 342 total gold precedents); Micro CR evaluates aggregate milestone citation. Entity CP (CPentity) evaluates whether
emitted citations resolve to authentic judicial opinions in the 20,000-case EEG index. Full TERRA has the lowest Entity CP (74.25% versus 89.10%–90.27% for the baselines). Doctrinal CP
(CPdoctrinal) evaluates whether emitted citations are grounded in the retrieved context or gold targets, weighted by the judge faithfulness score (Faithfulness ∈[0.0, 1.0]; canonical v2 evaluation file,
SHA256 prefix 95e775e2); it is a judge-weighted measure, not an NLI entailment test, and penalizes holding misattributions (e.g., transposing Baker and Reynolds in D033/D034). Full Paths Cited
reports the number of queries where 100% of the gold precedent chain was cited in the generated answer. On Category C unanswerable probes (N = 75, |Tq| = 0), precision is defined via the piecewise rule:
CP(q) = 1.00 if | ˆCq| = 0 (correct null emission / safe epistemic silence), and CP(q) = 0.00 if | ˆCq| > 0 (hallucinatory emission), eliminating 0/0 division. †The 4 TERRA links that fail the link validator
(canonical run) comprise three page-padding errors in identifiers of in-corpus cases (e.g. 301_001-01 for 301_0001-01) and one bare /case/118 path. The validator accepts a link when its identifier exists in
the index or its caption matches an indexed title or alias; 644 of 664 links have an existing identifier as written. All 4 baselines emit zero interactive markdown links.
