M. Generative Citation Correctness, Doctrinal Support,
and Interactive Hyperlink Provenance
Legal validity depends on whether the generator cites
and attributes doctrine correctly in its final completions,
since language models commonly invent case names, cite
authorities for propositions they never established, or
hallucinate citations on unanswerable inquiries. Table XI
therefore evaluates citation fidelity in the synthesized
completions across the 350-query suite (1,750 evaluations
over 5 pipelines). Grounded doctrinal precision and F1 are
primary (TERRA 49.4%/53.3%, HippoRAG 46.7%/45.3%,
Flat Vector RAG 39.1%/40.4%), with a judge-weighted
variant as secondary (TERRA 9.30%, HippoRAG 9.06%;
weighted by dual-judge faithfulness). TERRA’s entity cita-
tion precision is 74.25% (earlier single-judge run 91.95%),
and 660 of 664 emitted links resolved (99.40%).
1) Category B Precedent Milestone Citation and Doc-
trinal Support: On Category B (N = 100 queries, 342 gold
authorities; Table XI, Panel A), Full TERRA has the high-
est macro citation recall, 57.85% (55.56% micro), ahead of
HippoRAG-Adapted (43.88%), Direct LLM (43.55%), Flat
Vector RAG (41.88%) and GraphRAG-Adapted (41.10%)
by 13.97 to 16.75 percentage points. Its judge-weighted
Doctrinal Citation Precision, 9.30%, exceeds HippoRAG-
Adapted (9.06%), Direct LLM (7.08%) and GraphRAG-
Adapted (6.29%) but is below Flat Vector RAG (14.15%),
the highest. Likewise Citation F1 is 16.02% for TERRA,
higher than HippoRAG-Adapted (15.02%; ratio 1.07×)
and Direct LLM (12.17%; ratio 1.32×) but lower than
Flat Vector RAG (21.15%; ratio 0.76×). TERRA cites
all gold milestones in 23/100 answers (23.00%), against
4/100 for HippoRAG, 2/100 for Flat, 1/100 for Direct
LLM and 0/100 for GraphRAG.
2) Dynamic Interactive Hyperlink Attribution and Epis-
temic Negative Containment: Across the suite (Supple-
ment Section S14, Panel B), TERRA emitted 664 inter-
active links (Section III-B5); 660/664 (99.40%) pass the
project’s validator, which accepts a link when its identifier
exists in the index or its caption matches an indexed
title or alias, with 97.44% title-to-node fidelity (including
aliases such as the Slaughter-House Cases resolving to
Butchers’ Benevolent Ass’n, 83_0036-01). The remaining
4 fail: three carry page-padding errors in identifiers of in-
corpus cases (e.g. 301_001-01 for 301_0001-01, NLRB v.
Jones & Laughlin; queries B073 and D038) and one is a
bare /case/118 path (query B075). A stricter identifier-
existence check passes 644 of 664 links (97.0%); the other
16 were accepted on caption match. All four baselines
emit zero hyperlinks (0), leaving their citations unlinked.
On the N = 75 unanswerable Category C probes, where
emitting no citation scores 1.00 and a hallucinated one
0.00, Flat Vector RAG hallucinated 102 citations (12.00%
precision) while Full TERRA emitted 0 (100.00%; 75/75
clean refusals); because 74 of these 75 probes had no
retrieved context, this containment largely reflects refusal
on empty retrieval, not entailment grading.
