\clearpage\noindent \textbf{Notes:} Evaluated on Category B ($N=100$, 342 gold precedents). Target coverage uses an exact CAP ID anywhere in context or a case-title prefix longer than four characters; complete recovery means every gold target matches, not independently verified connected-path recovery in Panels A/B, on stored \texttt{context\_full} fields from \texttt{terra\_canonical\_v2\_eval.json} (SHA256 prefix \texttt{95e775e2}) delivered to the 70B generator in the primary dual-judge benchmark.
$^\dagger$The 5-node stratum ($N=5$) is a descriptive diagnostic demonstrating topological feasibility (exact Clopper-Pearson 95\% CI: \CatBFiveNodeClopperPearsonCI).
Panel C uses anchors extracted from \texttt{terra\_baselines\_eval\_raw.json}, not canonical-v2 contexts. Section 1 matches the older records' context-node sets, traversal-node sets and gold intersections on 100/100 queries (62/100 complete recoveries); this is set-level parity, not byte-identical context or fresh vector-retrieval reproduction. Section 2 uses the same legacy-derived anchors with a bounded engine and yields 61/100, distinct from canonical v2's 66/100. Section 2 evaluates the bounded post-Issue \#13 engine as an operational-efficiency diagnostic; visited nodes counts BFS expansion centers (mean \IssueTwentySixBoundMeanVisitedNodes), which reach the 35,000-character ceiling after expanding the 2 seeds, assembling \IssueTwentySixBoundMeanFullNodes\ unique case authorities into context and retaining \IssueTwentySixGoldRetentionPct\ of gold landmark precedents while eliminating \IssueTwentySixContextReductionPct\ of context character volume. Traversal latency strictly reflects offline NetworkX DiGraph traversal and string assembly on CPU. $^\mathsection$Bounded retrieval coverage is invariant across $d \in \{1,2,3\}$ and $d_{\text{adapt}}$ because character limits are reached at depth 1. $^\ast$Single-seed condition ($k=1^\ast$) initializes traversal strictly from the primary anchor. $^\ddagger$Vector-seed condition ($k=4^\ast$) queries ChromaDB under the known Issue \#19 taxonomy constraint (\texttt{civil\_rights}, 0 seeds).
