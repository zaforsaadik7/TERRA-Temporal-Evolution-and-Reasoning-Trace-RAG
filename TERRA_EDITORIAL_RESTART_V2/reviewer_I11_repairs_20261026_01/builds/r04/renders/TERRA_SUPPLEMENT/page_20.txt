S4.2
Source checks and sampling limits
The source-check frame was frozen using 40 distinct matched queries, 10 per category, selected once without replacement from sorted IDs with seed 42; both arms yield 80
outcomes. Inclusion probabilities are 1/10 for A/B and 2/15 for C/D. This is a post-hoc audit of already exposed data, not a new blinded human study or preregistration.
The probability component is kept separate from targeted historical fatal cases, positive-category refusal flags and external adverse records. No unweighted population error
rate is calculated from the combined set.
The audit checks actual verifier-message document identities, titles, dates and normalized Holding/Snippet spans against preserved CAP casebody material. Normalization
covers Unicode typography and whitespace, not paraphrase equivalence. Locating a span in a reporter headnote, majority opinion or other opinion is a source-location fact,
not certification that it is the Court’s holding. Unlocated text is not automatically fabricated: cleaning, truncation and unavailable sources remain possible. Candidate
quotation-shaped strings are flagged for location checks without assuming they quote a court. Source-date checks against explicit as-of-year phrases do not prove answer
leakage or controlling-law applicability.
Across the 80 sampled arm inputs, 948 document occurrences were parsed; primary CAP files were available for 948 occurrences. There were 0 title mismatches and 0 date
mismatches under the stated exact metadata comparison. Of 1896 normalized Holding/Snippet span checks, 1873 were located and 23 were not located. These occurrence
counts are descriptive provenance checks, not independent case counts or legal-accuracy estimates. The separate targeted ledger accounts for 20 historical records and 669
external adverse-flag records, with unavailable identity/interpretation recorded rather than guessed.
These mechanical source checks do not adjudicate complete answer correctness, disputed doctrine or the suﬀiciency of the evidence for each legal task. Existing assessor
decisions are preserved; no new human ratings are attributed to the authors. Unresolved source identity and legal interpretation remain explicit unknowns. The prior workbook
Column N/O correction task is not silently reopened.
S4.3
Verifier error-rate feasibility
All 700 matched verifier inputs survive as exact HTTP request bodies. Their raw UTF-8 SHA-256 values match both the recorded request event and accounting reservation;
query text matches the frozen benchmark. The request bodies, rather than the verifier’s own reason or later reconstructed graph text, define the audited inputs. There is one
final verifier decision per matched arm; unlogged historical retry states cannot be recovered from final contexts.
An independently supported reference on actual query/context suﬀiciency is still absent. Source-span presence, benchmark answerability, the verifier’s explanation and
a judge’s high score for abstention are not that reference. For example, B001’s T response receives faithfulness 1.0 for abstaining; that judgment alone establishes neither
justified refusal nor false rejection. Consequently, verifier false-acceptance and false-rejection rates are not estimated. If a valid reference becomes available, false rejection
divides rejected suﬀicient contexts by all reference-suﬀicient contexts; false acceptance divides passed insuﬀicient contexts by all reference-insuﬀicient contexts. Unsupported
among accepted answers is a different conditional outcome. No confusion matrix, legal-expert validation or counterfactual answer for a blocked generator is fabricated.
The existing author-rating proxies remain unchanged. In particular, Author 2’s six uncertain non-refusals remain separate: contradicted-among-non-refusals is 12/65, with
a descriptive unresolved-label envelope 12/65 to 18/65; this is not a population confidence interval. Original/reconciled source ratings, automated faithfulness, response-type
proxies and factual source-span checks answer different questions and are not pooled. The expert-assessment part of the reviewer request remains unfulfilled; this narrower
audit documents observable outcomes and unavailable validation rather than claiming legal reliability.
