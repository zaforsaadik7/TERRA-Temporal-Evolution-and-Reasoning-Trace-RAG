E. Exploratory Author Assessment
As an exploratory assessment, two project authors, nei-
ther legally trained nor licensed to practise law, reviewed
76 assigned response instances each with AI assistance. Au-
thor 1 drafted assessments and used AI for source finding,
interpretation, checking and rewriting; Author 2 reviewed
and amended AI-proposed ratings and rationales. Original
ratings were recorded before access to the other author’s
ratings; reconciliation subsequently incorporated shared
ratings and source-audit feedback. The sample includes
repeated questions and overlapping response instances,
so the assignments are not 152 independent queries.
These assessments are descriptive, not independent legal-
expert validation or population estimates. Definitions
and original-versus-reconciled descriptive summaries are
reported in the supplementary material.
