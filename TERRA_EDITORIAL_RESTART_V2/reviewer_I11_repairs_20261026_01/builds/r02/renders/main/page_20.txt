M. Generative Citation Correctness, Doctrinal Support,
and Interactive Hyperlink Provenance
Legal validity depends on whether the generator cites
and attributes doctrine correctly in its final completions,
since language models commonly invent case names, cite
authorities for propositions they never established, or
hallucinate citations on unanswerable inquiries. Table XI
therefore evaluates citation fidelity in the synthesized
completions across the 350-query suite (1,750 evaluations
over 5 pipelines). Grounded doctrinal precision and F1 are
primary (TERRA 49.4%/53.3%, HippoRAG 46.7%/45.3%,
Flat Vector RAG 39.1%/40.4%), with a judge-weighted
variant as secondary (TERRA 9.30%, HippoRAG 9.06%;
weighted by dual-judge faithfulness). TERRA’s entity cita-
tion precision is 74.25% (earlier single-judge run 91.95%),
and 660 of 664 emitted links resolved (99.40%).
1) Category B Precedent Milestone Citation and Doc-
trinal Support: On Category B (N = 100 queries, 342
gold authorities; Table XI, Panel A), Full TERRA has
the highest macro citation recall, 57.85% (55.56% mi-
cro), ahead of HippoRAG-Adapted (43.88%), Direct LLM
(43.55%), Flat Vector RAG (41.88%) and GraphRAG-
Adapted (41.10%) by +13.97% to +16.75% points. Its
judge-weighted Doctrinal Citation Precision, 9.30%, ex-
ceeds HippoRAG-Adapted (9.06%), Direct LLM (7.08%)
and GraphRAG-Adapted (6.29%) but is below Flat Vector
RAG (14.15%), the highest. Likewise Citation F1 is 16.02%
for TERRA, higher than HippoRAG-Adapted (15.02%;
ratio 1.07×) and Direct LLM (12.17%; ratio 1.32×)
but lower than Flat Vector RAG (21.15%; ratio 0.76×).
TERRA cites all gold milestones in 23/100 answers
(23.00%), against 4/100 for HippoRAG, 2/100 for Flat,
1/100 for Direct LLM and 0/100 for GraphRAG.
2) Dynamic Interactive Hyperlink Attribution and Epis-
temic Negative Containment: Across the suite (Panel B),
TERRA emitted 664 interactive links (Section III-B5);
660/664 (99.40%) pass the project’s validator, which
accepts a link when its identifier exists in the index
or its caption matches an indexed title or alias, with
97.44% title-to-node fidelity (including aliases such as the
Slaughter-House Cases resolving to Butchers’ Benevolent
Ass’n, 83_0036-01). The remaining 4 fail: three carry
page-padding errors in identifiers of in-corpus cases (e.g.
301_001-01 for 301_0001-01, NLRB v. Jones & Laughlin;
queries B073 and D038) and one is a bare /case/118 path
(query B075). A stricter identifier-existence check passes
644 of 664 links (97.0%); the other 16 were accepted on
caption match. All four baselines emit zero hyperlinks
(0), leaving their citations unlinked. On the N = 75
unanswerable Category C probes, where emitting no cita-
tion scores 1.00 and a hallucinated one 0.00, Flat Vector
RAG hallucinated 102 citations (12.00% precision) while
Full TERRA emitted 0 (100.00%; 75/75 clean refusals);
because 74 of these 75 probes had no retrieved context,
this containment largely reflects refusal on empty retrieval,
not entailment grading.
N. Computational Budget Profiling and Geometric Gate
Calibration
Multi-stage pipelines compound latency, tokens and
cost, so Table XII profiles operations per query, active
70B generator calls, stage and end-to-end latency, token
volumes and modelled cost for all 5 architectures, together
with a distance-only diagnostic of the Tier 2 proximity flag
on the 20,000-case index.
1) Sequential Pipeline Operations and Call Parity: The
retrieval baselines run a two-stage retrieve-then-generate
pipeline (2.00 operations per query) with 350(100.0%)
generator calls over the N = 350 queries. Direct LLM
performs generation only. TERRA runs q →Router →
Retrieval/BFS →NLI Grader →70B Generator and
performs 1,261 operations (3.60 per query; counted where
a stage recorded non-zero time) at a modelled $5.12 per
1,000 queries (Gemma 3 12B router; Llama 3.3 70B grader
and generator). The cost is modelled from token estimates
at list prices, not metered; it prices a grader call for every
Category B–D query (twice for refused Category C and D
queries) and none for Category A, although Category A
recorded grading time. Pricing one grader call only where
recorded grading time was at least 1 ms gives $4.66 per
1,000 queries.
