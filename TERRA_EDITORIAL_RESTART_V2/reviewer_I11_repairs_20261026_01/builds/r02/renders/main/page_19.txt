1) Precedent-Chain Recovery and Chain-Length Disag-
gregation: Flat Vector RAG reaches 26.8% macro node
recall (26.3% micro) and recovers the complete trajectory
for only 1.0% of queries (1/100): dense embeddings find
one or two lexically adjacent landmarks but miss interme-
diate stepping-stones. GraphRAG-Adapted reaches 35.7%
node recall and 9.0% full-path recovery (9/100), since
static Louvain communities group cases topically with-
out chronology. HippoRAG-Adapted has substantially
higher node recall (68.5%) but only 21.0% full-path recall
(21/100), as undirected Personalized PageRank spreads
mass over high-degree peripheral precedents. Full TERRA
reaches 88.9% macro node recall (88.3% micro) and recov-
ers the full chain on 66/100 queries, a 62.1-percentage-
point node-recall difference and 66× path-recovery factor
over Flat Vector RAG (1/100), and a 20.4-percentage-
point node-recall difference and 3.1× path-recovery factor
over HippoRAG.
By chain length (Panel B): on the N = 63 3-node chains
(2 hops), full-path recovery is 1.6% for Flat (1/63), 14.3%
for GraphRAG (9/63), 31.7% for HippoRAG (20/63) and
77.8% for TERRA (49/63; node recall 91.5%). On the N =
32 4-node chains (3 hops), Flat (0.0%) and GraphRAG
(0.0%) recover none and HippoRAG 3.1% (1/32), against
TERRA’s 50.0% (16/32; node recall 85.2%). On the N = 5
5-node chains (4 hops), all baselines recover 0/5 and
TERRA 20.0% (1/5; node recall 80.0%; exact 95% CI
[0.5%, 71.6%]), a stratum too small for more than a
descriptive diagnostic.
2) Multi-Seed Anchoring: For an evolutionary query q,
entity and legal-concept extraction yields seeds Sq ⊂V
with |Sq| ≥2 (mean 2.41 per Category B query; 73.0%
with two, 19.0% with three, 8.0% with four or more),
and each seed expands a d = 2 ball along forward and
backward citation edges with breadth k = 2. Multi-
seed extraction is designed to anchor both extremities
of a trajectory (index 0 and m −1); seed placement
was not re-audited for the canonical run, so this is a
mechanism explanation, not a measured result. For a
chain v0
→· · · →vm−1, the forward ball B(v0, 2)
covers {v0, v1, v2} and the reverse ball B(vm−1, 2) covers
{vm−1, vm−2, vm−3}, so when branching stays within the
beam the two neighborhoods meet at intermediate author-
ities and can cover paths of up to four hops without deeper
search. In the canonical run all gold nodes were recovered
for 16/32 4-node and 1/5 5-node chains; high fan-out
exceeding the beam is a plausible but untested explanation
for the 16 incompletely recovered 4-node chains. We did
not test causally whether TERRA’s results (Tables IV
and IX) stem from retrieval quality; Direct LLM’s higher
Category B faithfulness shows retrieval alone does not
explain faithfulness.
