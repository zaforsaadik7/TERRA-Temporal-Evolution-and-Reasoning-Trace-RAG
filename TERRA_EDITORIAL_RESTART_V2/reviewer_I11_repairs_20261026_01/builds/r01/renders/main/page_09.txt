detailed analysis via candidate length, and scores re-

(r = 0.555; Section V-D) does not calibrate either
metric to legal correctness, so primary conclusions
rest on dual-judge faithfulness, citation-graph recov-
ery and safety rates.
• Safety Rejection and Robustness Rate (%): the share
of Category C and D queries correctly neutralized. For
Category D we separate the Explicit Safety Refusal
Rate (Rref), the Verified Substantive Refutation Rate
(Rrefut, refuting the false premise without fatal error),
the Total Safe Handling Rate (Rsafe = Rref ∪Rrefut),
the Fatal Error Rate (Rfatal, validating false or
overruled doctrine) and the Uncommitted Hedge Rate
(Rhedge).
• Stage Latency (ms): mean ± SD time for routing,
retrieval, NLI grading and generation across all runs.
