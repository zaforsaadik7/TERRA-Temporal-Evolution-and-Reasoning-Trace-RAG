2) Deterministic Procedural Substrate (N = 20, 000,
100.0%): all 20,000 opinions are serialized by a
procedural function (build_structured_trace) from
the CAP case title (name_abbreviation), decision
year (decision_date) and a domain phrase. Every
node receives the same five-step template (legal
question, governing precedent, factual record, harmo-
nizing doctrines, holding), with no case-specific issue,
procedural history, test or holding. The domain
phrase differs from the node’s category field for
18,247 of 20,000 nodes (19,298 read “Civil Rights
Equal Protection”, although 1,153 nodes carry that
category). The evaluated index supplies generic pro-
cedural scaffolds, not LLM-generated case-specific
reasoning.
