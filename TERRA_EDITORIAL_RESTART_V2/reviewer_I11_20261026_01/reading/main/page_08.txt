a) Obscure Overruled Precedent Suite.:
To test
whether Category D safety reflects retrieval or memoriza-
tion of famous landmarks, we built a supplementary suite
of 30 obscure overruled-precedent queries from the 47 CRS
OVERRULES edges not used in Category D, excluding
three whose overruling opinions never mention the target
(we infer, without a retained exclusion ledger, that these
are the three mis-resolved edges of Section III-A) and
selecting the thirty lowest-in-degree pairs by a mechanical
rule fixed before inference. Each query asks what the
overruled decision held without hinting at the overruling
authority (leakage audited). Four arms ran under identical
settings: Direct LLM; Flat Vector RAG with the identical
instruction block; Full TERRA; and TERRA-Unlabeled,
which removes the relation tags, the overruling heading,
the prompt’s reference to the tag and the enlarged excerpt
allocation for overruling edges while keeping seeds, traver-
sal and router unchanged. Two judges (Qwen3-Next-80B,
GPT-OSS-120B; neither is the generator) classified each
answer as refusal, verified refutation, fatal error (aﬀirmed
as current law, or recited without warning) or hedge,
and the combined class takes the more severe judgment.
The primary hypothesis, sensitivity subsets and cross-suite
contrast were preregistered before any live output; for
the contrast we re-ran the nineteen Category D doctrine-
invalidation queries under identical code, prompts and
judges.
B. Comparative Baselines
TERRA is benchmarked against four representative
baseline architectures spanning parametric and retrieval-
augmented paradigms: (1) Direct LLM (No RAG), where
query q is passed directly to the generator without
retrieval, evaluating raw parametric knowledge and un-
gated hallucination risks; (2) Flat Vector RAG, where
ChromaDB retrieves top-k scaffold-bearing opinion doc-
uments by semantic embedding similarity (nresults = 2)
and passes them directly to the generator without citation
graph traversal or NLI entailment grading; (3) GraphRAG-
Adapted (Louvain implementation), adapting Microsoft
GraphRAG [5] by partitioning opinion entities into Lou-
vain graph communities (Q = 0.6110) and generating
responses from local subgraph plus parent community sum-
maries, omitting directed chronological precedent edges;
and (4) HippoRAG-Adapted, adapting hippocampal mem-
ory indexing [26] by constructing a symmetric bipartite
citation graph (290,706 non-zero entries) and executing
Personalized PageRank (PPR) associative diffusion seeded
from query entity mentions, without typed directional
citation constraints or downstream NLI self-correction
gates.
