2) Dynamic Interactive Hyperlink Attribution and Epis-
temic Negative Containment: Across the suite (Panel B),
TERRA emitted 664 interactive links (Section III-B5);
660/664 (99.40%) pass the project’s validator, which

or its caption matches an indexed title or alias, with

(0), leaving their citations unlinked. On the N = 75
