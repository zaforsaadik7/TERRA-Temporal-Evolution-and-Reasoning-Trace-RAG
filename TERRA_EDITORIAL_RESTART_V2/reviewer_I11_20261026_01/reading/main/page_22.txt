2) Latency and Cost Trade-Offs:
On Category B,
TERRA has a median latency of 17.94 s (26, 298.0 ms
mean) and a modelled cost of $11.74 per 1,000 queries
($0.0117 per query), against 2, 952.2 ms and $0.54 per
1,000 ($0.00054 per query) for Flat Vector RAG, a
22× per-query cost ratio. We disclose this overhead as
deliberate: Flat Vector RAG recovers the full precedent
path on only 1.0% of queries (1/100) against TERRA’s
66.0% (66/100; Section VI-L), and the measured retrieval
gain is accompanied by about 17.94 s latency and $0.0117
per query (modelled); these results do not establish a de-
fensible deployment tradeoff or malpractice-risk reduction.
