C. Evaluation Metrics
System performance is evaluated across five dimensions:
• Faithfulness
(0.0–1.0):
the
mean
of
two
LLM
judges [27], databricks-qwen3-next-80b-a3b-instruct
(Judge 1) and databricks-gpt-oss-120b (Judge 2), each
scoring a five-point Likert rubric mapped to (L−1)/4.
Neither is the Llama-3.3-70B model that serves as
generator, NLI grader and Category D verifier.
• Relevance (0.0–1.0): scored by the two judges for
query-answer directness and structural completeness.
• ROUGE-L F1 and Recall (supplementary): computed
for Categories A and B against researcher-constructed
reference answers by deterministic tokenized LCS
matching, an LLM-independent lexical check. Ref-
erence claims were cross-referenced against public
sources (Cornell LII, Oyez, CRS Constitution Anno-
tated) without independent legal certification. Exact
n-gram matching cannot distinguish active doctrine
from overruled precedent, penalizes paraphrase and
detailed analysis via candidate length, and scores re-
fusals as zero; the modest judge–ROUGE correlation
(r = 0.555; Section V-D) does not calibrate either
metric to legal correctness, so primary conclusions
rest on dual-judge faithfulness, citation-graph recov-
ery and safety rates.
• Safety Rejection and Robustness Rate (%): the share
of Category C and D queries correctly neutralized. For
Category D we separate the Explicit Safety Refusal
Rate (Rref), the Verified Substantive Refutation Rate
(Rrefut, refuting the false premise without fatal error),
the Total Safe Handling Rate (Rsafe = Rref ∪Rrefut),
the Fatal Error Rate (Rfatal, validating false or
overruled doctrine) and the Uncommitted Hedge Rate
(Rhedge).
• Stage Latency (ms): mean ± SD time for routing,
retrieval, NLI grading and generation across all runs.
