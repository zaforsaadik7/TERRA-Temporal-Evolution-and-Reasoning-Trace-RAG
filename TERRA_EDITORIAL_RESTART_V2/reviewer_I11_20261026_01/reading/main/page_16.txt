Notes: Evaluated across 120 completion instances (N = 30 obscure overruled queries across evaluated pipelines) dual-judged by
independent models (qwen3-next and gpt-oss, κ = 0.5746, unverified quote hallucinations: 16 and 26). Safe completions comprise Rref
(aﬀirmative recognition that precedent was overruled) and Rrefut (correct substantive refutation of query proposition under modern
doctrine). Unsafe completions comprise Fatal-Aﬀirmed (active endorsement of overruled holding) and Fatal-Unwarned (silent reliance
without caveat). Rhedge denotes non-committal or generic procedural disclaimers. Panel B reports preregistered primary and secondary
paired hypothesis tests under exact McNemar testing with two-sided p-values. Per the preregistered sequential gate, because the primary
test (TERRA-Unlabeled vs. Flat Vector RAG) is not rejected (p = 0.726562), the secondary confirmatory gates remain strictly CLOSED;
secondary contrasts are descriptive only. Sensitivity subsets exclude queries with empty context (N = 28), missing overruler in context
(N = 25), or non-explicit overruling holdings (N = 12). Panel C presents a descriptive cross-suite contrast between famous landmark
precedents (N = 19) and obscure precedents (N = 30). Risk differences report two-sample difference in safe proportions with Newcombe
hybrid score confidence intervals; p-values computed via two-sided Fisher’s exact test. Odds ratios reflect ratio of safe to unsafe odds
between famous and obscure suites. Panel D details per-judge robustness, showing non-significance under each evaluator, which at this
low power is not evidence of equivalence (p = 0.092285 under Judge One and p = 1.000000 under Judge Two). ∗Haldane-Anscombe
zero-cell continuity correction applied.
e) Cross-suite contrast (descriptive).: Full TERRA’s
safe handling was 19/30 (63.3%) on obscure precedents
versus 14/19 (73.7%) on the famous subset (risk difference
+10.3%, 95% CI [−16.6%, +33.3%]); Direct LLM and
TERRA-Unlabeled fell from 15/19 (78.9%) and 15/19
(78.9%) to 0/30 (0.0%) and 8/30 (26.7%). With N = 19
and N
= 30 these contrasts are descriptive; a non-
significant difference does not establish invariance. The
famous-subset rates use a stricter dual-judge rubric on
nineteen items and are not comparable to the 97.3% safe-
handling rate of Table V.
f) Reliability.: Inter-judge agreement was Cohen’s
κ = 0.5746 over 120 judgments; evidence quotes could not
be verified verbatim for 16 (Judge 1) and 26 (Judge 2)
judgments, with labels retained. Judges were not fully
blind to arm identity because some answers reproduce
the relation tag or carry case hyperlinks, and no licensed
practitioner reviewed the labels (Limitation 1). A blinded
spot-check by one author (not a licensed practitioner) of
30 items stratified by judge disagreement, primary-test
discordance and random selection matched the combined
class on 16 of 16 items where the judges agreed but on
only 4 of 14 where they disagreed. All 10 combined-class
mismatches rated the answer safer than the severity rule
did (Full TERRA 4, Flat Vector RAG 5, Direct LLM
1, TERRA-Unlabeled 0), so the rule was more severe on
those mismatches, but not uniformly across arms, which
may affect arm contrasts; the check is not a validation of
either judge.
