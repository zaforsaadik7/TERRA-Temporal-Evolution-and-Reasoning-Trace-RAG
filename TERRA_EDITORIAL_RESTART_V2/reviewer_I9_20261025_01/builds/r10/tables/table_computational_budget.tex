\raggedright \fontsize{8.5}{10.5}\selectfont \textbf{Notes:} Computational profiling and stage latencies across $N=350$ queries from the canonical v2 evaluation (\texttt{data/terra\_canonical\_v2\_eval.json}). Operations per query count each stage (routing, retrieval, NLI grading, generation) that recorded non-zero time. Generator calls count queries with non-zero generation time. Routing, NLI grading and generation are means of the logged timing fields. Retrieval is the logged retrieval time minus routing time for hard-route queries (whose retrieval timer includes routing) and the logged value otherwise. The stages sum to the end-to-end time for Category~A and the single-shot baselines; for Categories~B--D a remainder not attributed to any stage averages \TerraCatBRemainderMS, \TerraCatCRemainderMS\ and \TerraCatDRemainderMS\ ms. Category~A recorded retrieval and NLI-grader time on every query. Category~C recorded no generation; for 74 of 75 queries the context was empty and the recorded grading time was under 1~ms. Panel B is an offline distance-only calibration; no query was stopped before retrieval in the canonical run. Its in-domain false-positive labels are not observed deployed false refusals. Costs are modelled, not metered: token counts are estimated from character or word counts, Gemma 3 12B routing is priced at \$0.15/1M tokens and LLaMA 3.3 70B grading and generation at \$0.60/1M tokens, a grader call is priced for every Category~B--D query (twice for refused Category~C and~D queries) and none for Category~A, and retrieval is priced at \$0.00. Pricing one grader call only where the recorded grading time was at least 1~ms gives \$4.66 per 1,000 queries overall instead of \TerraDeployedCostPerThousand.
