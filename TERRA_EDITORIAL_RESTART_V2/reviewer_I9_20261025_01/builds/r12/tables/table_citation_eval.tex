\raggedright \fontsize{8.5}{10.5}\selectfont \textbf{Notes:} Evaluates citation correctness and explainable AI attribution directly in the final generated text across the canonical 350-benchmark suite (1,750 comparative completions). 
\textbf{Macro CR} measures the average per-query recall of gold precedent milestones in Category B ($N=100$, 342 total gold precedents); \textbf{Micro CR} evaluates aggregate milestone citation. 
\textbf{Entity CP} ($\text{CP}_{\text{entity}}$) evaluates whether emitted citations resolve to authentic judicial opinions in the 20,000-case EEG index. Full TERRA has the lowest Entity CP (\CatBTerraEntityCitationPrecision{} versus \CatBGraphRAGEntityCitationPrecision{}--\CatBDirectEntityCitationPrecision{} for the baselines). 
\textbf{Doctrinal CP} ($\text{CP}_{\text{doctrinal}}$) evaluates whether emitted citations are grounded in the retrieved context or gold targets, weighted by the judge faithfulness score ($\text{Faithfulness} \in [0.0, 1.0]$; canonical v2 evaluation file, SHA256 prefix \texttt{95e775e2}); it is a judge-weighted measure, not an NLI entailment test, and penalizes holding misattributions (e.g., transposing \textit{Baker} and \textit{Reynolds} in D033/D034). 
\textbf{Full Paths Cited} reports the number of queries where 100\% of the gold precedent chain was cited in the generated answer. 
On Category C unanswerable probes ($N=75$, $|T_q|=0$), precision is defined via the piecewise rule: $\text{CP}(q) = 1.00$ if $|\hat{C}_q|=0$ (correct null emission / safe epistemic silence), and $\text{CP}(q) = 0.00$ if $|\hat{C}_q|>0$ (hallucinatory emission), eliminating $0/0$ division. 
$^\dagger$The \TerraBrokenMarkdownLinks{} TERRA links that fail the link validator (canonical run) comprise three page-padding errors in identifiers of in-corpus cases (e.g.\ \texttt{301\_001-01} for \texttt{301\_0001-01}) and one bare \texttt{/case/118} path. The validator accepts a link when its identifier exists in the index or its caption matches an indexed title or alias; 644 of 664 links have an existing identifier as written. All 4 baselines emit zero interactive markdown links.
