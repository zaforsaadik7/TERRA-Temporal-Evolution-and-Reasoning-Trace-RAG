$^{**}$Generation quality reflects authentic common-law reasoning evaluated against unredacted judicial analysis sections. Citation metrics parsed via \texttt{eyecite} report Citation $F_1$ alongside component Citation Precision (CP) and Citation Recall (CR).
$^\dagger$Disclosed Reasoning denotes completions that explicitly identify the query as requiring authorities outside the 1850--1980 historical precedent corpus and ground claims exclusively in validated historical doctrine without fabricating modern precedents.
Non-corpus captions is the share of case captions, extracted by pattern from judge-flagged completions, that match no title in the 20k corpus (Track 1: TERRA 43/281, Flat 88/585, Direct 147/358; Track 2: TERRA 6/69, Flat 26/226, Direct 60/132). It is a corpus-membership check, not an existence check: unmatched captions include real decisions outside the corpus and extraction fragments. An earlier recorded fabrication value of 0.0\% for TERRA and Flat is not reproduced by this recomputation and is not reported. Doctrinal Misapplication Rate is the judges' flag for doctrinal misapplication or hallucination, computed over all $N$ completions of each pipeline (gate-refused completions are not flagged; among TERRA's 67 generated Track 1 completions the rates are $\ClercTerraMisappGenOr$ under OR and $\ClercTerraMisappGenAnd$ under AND), reported under both Logical OR (conservative union) and Unanimous AND (dual-judge consensus). Citation $F_1$ is computed from micro-pooled CP and CR.
