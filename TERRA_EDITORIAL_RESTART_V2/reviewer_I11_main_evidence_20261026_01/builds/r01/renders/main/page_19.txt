L. Direct Evidence Retrieval Quality and Precedent-
Chain Coverage
Do downstream generative results reflect better evi-
dence retrieval, or only the generator’s ability to synthesize
plausible analyses? Because TERRA’s expansion uses
breadth k = 2 and depth d = 2 while Category B arcs
span 3 to 5 nodes (2 to 4 hops), we evaluate retrieval
as a function of chain length. To avoid conflating single-
point lookups (routed through anchor retrieval and entail-
ment grading without graph expansion) or unanswerable
probes, we score raw evidence retrieval on the N = 100
Category B trajectories (342 gold landmark precedents)
before generation. The four systems return heterogeneous
representations (ranked chunks, unranked community re-
ports, unranked PPR clusters, and a 2-hop directed EEG
subgraph), so ranked-list metrics such as MRR or nDCG
cannot be applied uniformly; we report deterministic dual-
resolution entity recall (CAP Node ID and canonical title)
and full-target coverage in Table X, computed on the
stored context_full fields of the canonical v2 file (SHA256
prefix 95e775e2) that the generator actually received. Here
“full-path recovery” denotes matching every gold target by
ID or title; connected-path recovery was not independently
verified.
1) Precedent-Chain Recovery and Chain-Length Disag-
gregation: Flat Vector RAG reaches 26.8% macro node
recall (26.3% micro) and recovers the complete trajectory
for only 1.0% of queries (1/100): dense embeddings find
one or two lexically adjacent landmarks but miss interme-
diate stepping-stones. GraphRAG-Adapted reaches 35.7%
node recall and 9.0% full-path recovery (9/100), since
static Louvain communities group cases topically with-
out chronology. HippoRAG-Adapted has substantially
higher node recall (68.5%) but only 21.0% full-path recall
(21/100), as undirected Personalized PageRank spreads
mass over high-degree peripheral precedents. Full TERRA
reaches 88.9% macro node recall (88.3% micro) and recov-
ers the full chain on 66/100 queries, a 62.1-percentage-
point node-recall difference and 66× path-recovery factor
over Flat Vector RAG (1/100), and a 20.4-percentage-
point node-recall difference and 3.1× path-recovery factor
over HippoRAG.
By chain length (Supplement Section S13, Panel B): on
the N = 63 3-node chains (2 hops), full-path recovery is
1.6% for Flat (1/63), 14.3% for GraphRAG (9/63), 31.7%
for HippoRAG (20/63) and 77.8% for TERRA (49/63;
node recall 91.5%). On the N = 32 4-node chains (3 hops),
Flat (0.0%) and GraphRAG (0.0%) recover none and

small for more than a descriptive diagnostic.
