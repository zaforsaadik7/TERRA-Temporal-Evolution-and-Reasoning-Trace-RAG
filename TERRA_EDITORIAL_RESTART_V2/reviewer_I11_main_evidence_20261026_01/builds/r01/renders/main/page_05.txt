3) Tier 3, Semantic Entailment Verification: because
proximity cannot verify authority or prevent misap-

subgraph, and if the permitted evidence checks fail,
generation ends in a deterministic safety refusal. In
the canonical run, 81 of the 139 refusals followed an

A fatal constraint violation is a response that aﬀirms
an overruled doctrine, misattributes controlling constitu-

from an adversarial prompt (Category D); a false refusal is
an unwarranted refusal of an in-domain Category A lookup
whose controlling precedent is present and unambiguous.
