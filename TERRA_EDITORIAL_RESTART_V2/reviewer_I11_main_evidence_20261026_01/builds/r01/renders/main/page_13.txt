Faithfulness. Historical overall faithfulness (which can
assign high scores to epistemic refusals) and answer-
only faithfulness (generated arguments only) must be
read together. TERRA has the highest overall faithful-
ness (0.695, 95% CI [0.654, 0.735]), ahead of HippoRAG-
Adapted (0.670, [0.627, 0.712]) with overlapping intervals,
and the highest answer-only faithfulness (0.586 vs. 0.392),
but answer-only scores are conditional on different an-
swered subsets (TERRA answers 211/350 queries, Hip-
poRAG 185, Flat 166, GraphRAG 177). Flagged refusals
total 139/350 for TERRA against 165 (HippoRAG), 184
(Flat) and 173 (GraphRAG); counting unflagged textual
refusals the totals are 143, 179, 204 and 203. Clamping
Category D refusals and verified refutations to at least
0.75 (Amendments K–M) would raise overall faithfulness
by 0.82 to 4.75 points across pipelines (TERRA 0.6950
raw, 0.7357 clamped) and answer-only faithfulness by
0.82 to 9.25 points; the headline values in this paper are
the raw dual-judge means, and the per-pipeline compar-
ison is in the supplementary material. On Category B
TERRA (0.254) significantly exceeds HippoRAG (0.188,
∆= +0.066, q = 0.0124), GraphRAG (0.135, ∆= +0.119,
q = 1.97×10−5) and Flat Vector RAG (0.184, ∆= +0.070,
q = 0.0063), whereas a non-retrieval Direct LLM scores
higher (0.335, ∆= −0.081, q = 0.0023; TERRA win rate
27.0%; Section VI-B). On Category A, TERRA scores
0.941 against HippoRAG 0.910, Flat 0.855 and Direct
LLM 0.514: significantly higher than Direct (+0.427) and
Flat (+0.086, q = 0.0189), and without a significant
difference from GraphRAG (+0.052, q = 0.1169) and
HippoRAG (+0.031, q = 0.3865). The gate falsely refuses
2/100 Category A and 16/100 Category B queries.
