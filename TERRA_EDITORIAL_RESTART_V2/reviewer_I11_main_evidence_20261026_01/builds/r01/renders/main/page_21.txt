N. Computational Budget Profiling and Geometric Gate
Calibration
Multi-stage pipelines compound latency, tokens and
cost, so Table XII profiles operations per query, active
70B generator calls, stage and end-to-end latency, token
volumes and modelled cost for all 5 architectures, together
with a distance-only diagnostic of the Tier 2 proximity flag
on the 20,000-case index.
1) Sequential Pipeline Operations and Call Parity: The
retrieval baselines run a two-stage retrieve-then-generate
pipeline (2.00 operations per query) with 350(100.0%)
generator calls over the N = 350 queries. Direct LLM
performs generation only. TERRA runs q →Router →
Retrieval/BFS →NLI Grader →70B Generator and
performs 1,261 operations (3.60 per query; counted where
a stage recorded non-zero time) at a modelled $5.12 per
1,000 queries (Gemma 3 12B router; Llama 3.3 70B grader
and generator). The cost is modelled from token estimates
at list prices, not metered; it prices a grader call for every
Category B–D query (twice for refused Category C and D
queries) and none for Category A, although Category A
recorded grading time. Pricing one grader call only where
recorded grading time was at least 1 ms gives $4.66 per
1,000 queries.
The counts come from recorded stage times. Category A
(the nominal fast path) recorded retrieval and grading
time on all 100 queries and invoked the generator on 98
(98.0%); Category B recorded all four stages and generated
on 84 (84.0%); Category C recorded routing, retrieval
and grading on all 75 queries but never generated (all
refused; for 74 the grading time was under 1 ms and
the context empty, consistent with a deterministic empty-
context rejection, not an LLM grading call); Category D
recorded all three pre-generation stages and generated on
29 (38.7%), the other 46 being refused before generation (7
with an empty context), and the generated textual refusals
D068 and D070 bring explicit refusals to 48. This gives
398, 384, 225 and 254 operations on Categories A–D (3.98,
3.84, 3.00 and 3.39 per query) and 1,261 in total (3.60
per query). Every Category C query recorded retrieval
time (at least 178 ms excluding routing), so no query was
terminated before retrieval and the threshold analysis in
Panel B calibrates a candidate distance-only diagnostic
rather than describing an early-refusal rule in the deployed
run.
TERRA invokes the generator on only 211 of 350 queries
(60.3%), a 39.7% reduction against the baselines’ 350/350,
but the reduction comes from refusals (Category C made
none, Category D 29 (38.7%), and some Category A and B
refusals also skip generation) and is not a reduction in 70B
use: the Llama 3.3 70B grader recorded at least 1 ms on
276 of 350 queries, so generator and grader calls together
exceed the baselines’ one call per query.
