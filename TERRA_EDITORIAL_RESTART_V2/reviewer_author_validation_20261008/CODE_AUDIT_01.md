## Blocking findings and concrete repair

1. **Biased usefulness sample / wrong inclusion probability.** Preparation lines 446–476 exclude diagnostic canonical cases before selecting 40, including D033/D034, yet assign probability 40/350 and label an SRS of the 350 population. Draw the probability sample FIRST from all 350; then select targeted diagnostics from remaining records. If a known adverse case already occurs in SRS, keep its SRS membership and flag privately as a diagnostic case study too without duplicate population counting. Diagnose at most 12, not necessarily exactly 12. Never remove a sampled adverse case to meet nominal workload.

2. **FNR and false omission are not computed.** Processor lines 389–405 set FNR to literal 0.0000 whenever human-positive cases exist, and call human leakage prevalence over all 80 responses a false-omission rate. Mapping never retains the original automated classification needed to distinguish FN from TP. Store that classification, validity and operational definition privately and join only after annotations lock. Compute FN/(TP+FN) and FN/(TN+FN) on valid paired labels respectively, with missing/uncertain cases explicit. Test 10 currently EXPECTS the erroneous zero; replace it with examples containing actual FN and TP, all-negative, undefined and missing-automated-label cases.

3. **Claimed cluster uncertainty/sensitivity absent.** Processor applies independent-binomial CP to 80 correlated temporal responses, drops inclusion probabilities from merged records and never calculates promised second-author sensitivity bounds. No cluster bootstrap exists despite the docstring. Provide stratified query-cluster design-aware estimates and uncertainty, pipeline/condition estimands, and overlap-label sensitivity; or explicitly defer analysis rather than delivering falsely named statistics. Freeze target automated classification (e.g. Level-2 leakage, not any future-case mention) consistently with the author rubric.

4. **Incomplete submissions can produce perfect agreement and plausible rates.** No required label-domain/source/rationale/timestamp validation. Blank overlaps yield agreement_percent=100 and kappa=1. Constant-category kappa is undefined, not 1. Blanks/invalid strings must not be treated as negative or not-applicable labels. Validate exact enums, semantic combinations, required evidence with justified exceptions and completion; incomplete files may have a completion report but must not produce finalized metrics. No substantive answers means contradicted-among-answered is undefined, not 0. Supported-and-responsive must use the predefined usefulness definition, not silently include partial answers. Use exact category matching rather than substrings.

5. **Assessor/category and pipeline-order confounding.** Sorting query IDs then splitting first/last 20 puts earlier categories with author 1 and later categories with author 2. Temporal allocation similarly follows sorted IDs and overlap is only the first cutoff. Randomize/balance primary author allocation independently of labels, randomize blinded order within temporal clusters so four fixed pipeline positions are not identifiable, and distribute/select overlap prospectively across represented strata. Condition choice by sorted rank requires an explicit target/inclusion-probability argument or balanced random assignment after query selection. Keep cluster identifiers but no recoverable pipeline ordering.

6. **Workbook not yet adequate for source reading.** Every answer row is fixed at height 45; responses of thousands of characters are visually clipped. The continuation sheet only helps above 30000 characters, leaving ordinary long answers inaccessible in a convenient shareable reading view. Provide full, comfortably readable per-item sheets/continuation layout and blank assessment fields; verify rendered or actual workbook viewing before delivery. Query text needs the same long-text preservation as answers. Provide semantic rubric definitions (not only allowed-value lists), cutoff/domain criteria for canonical questions, rationale and pinpoint source fields, and the genuinely separate context-support second pass. Existing instructions call three steps a two-pass workflow without the specified lock/context reveal. No forced reading of hidden/truncated cells.
