## Author 1 item 032 — supported core, potentially misleading qualification

The Daniel Ball quotation corresponds to page 563. The 1874 Montello majority reaffirms the navigability framework and concludes that Fox River forms an interstate highway through its connection to Green Bay. However, describing its requirement as an `unbroken water link` is potentially misleading if understood to require uninterrupted vessel navigation. Pages 440–443 discuss Durham boats, portages and natural obstructions and reject the lower court's restrictive test.

Proposed wording, not applied: replace `an unbroken water link` with `a continued highway for interstate or foreign commerce, by itself or through connection with other waters`; add that navigability in fact does not require an unobstructed route for modern vessels. This needs scope review, not an automatic rating change. Include pages 440–443 for the obstacle/portage point, beyond the entry's existing pinpoints.
