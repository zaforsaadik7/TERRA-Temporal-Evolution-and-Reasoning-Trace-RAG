# How to complete your assessment

Each author receives a separate workbook and matching HTML/TXT reading companions. Complete only your own assigned file.

1. Read `Instructions_and_Rubric` in the workbook before starting. The files contain model answers to evaluate, not verified legal answers.
2. Open your matching `READING_VIEW_AUTHOR_1.html` or `READING_VIEW_AUTHOR_2.html` in a browser for full answers. The TXT file is a fallback. Find the same Assessment ID in the workbook's `Assessments` sheet.
3. Fill columns G through Q. Use the exact category wording in the rubric; the `[Select]` headings do not guarantee a dropdown. Record primary-source citation/URL, supporting excerpt, rationale, your name/role and completion time. Use uncertain when evidence cannot support a definite judgment. Never invent a source.
4. Do not change columns A through F, IDs, query text, cutoff years, response text or reading views. If you notice a file problem, report the ID separately.
5. Work independently. Do not discuss item judgments or view the other author's file until both first-pass submissions are returned and locked. Do not use automated judges to supply your judgments.
6. Return your completed XLSX. Do not fill both the XLSX and TSV versions; the XLSX is the designated submission. No need to return unchanged reading companions.

This is a source-grounded review by the authors, not licensed legal-expert validation. Context/verifier review is a later second pass, after these initial judgments are locked.
