\section{Useful-answer and verifier-reference audit}
\label{supp:utility-audit}
This post-hoc audit keeps pipeline events, response-type judgments and source-supported correctness separate. It reuses the fresh matched comparison's 350 query pairs, the historical 1,750 arm records and the existing author ratings without generating new answers or changing scores. The author assessment remains exploratory, AI-assisted and nonexpert; its original and reconciled values retain their separate 76-row denominators. The 152 assignments include overlapping responses and are not 152 independent queries. Those authors did not provide reference labels for the fresh matched answers. Supporting-quotation/rationale repairs remain excluded from the completed ratings-only assessment.

\subsection{Answering events and response-type proxies}
An operational verifier rejection omits the generator and emits an explicit inability-to-establish-answer prefix plus the verifier's explanation. Generation invocation does not guarantee a substantive answer. The two stored judges also classify the final text; their semantic labels can disagree, including on an operational refusal. We report both classifications without resolving them using faithfulness scores.

These are invocation and judge-label proxies, not validated substantive-answer coverage, useful-answer yield or legal accuracy. The one-judge/two-judge counts are separate observed label rules, not confidence limits on true coverage. No new significance test or change to the matched primary estimand is made.

For the full assigned cohort, the concordant-label partition is H:77 substantive,105 abstentions,4 mixed and 164 disagreements; T:29 substantive,201 abstentions,2 mixed,2 premise refutations and 116 disagreements. Disagreement remains unclassifiable; a high abstention faithfulness score is not counted as a correct substantive answer. A justified premise refutation or refusal may be an appropriate task response, but is distinct from answering an answerable question. Missing or uncertain support is not recoded as wrong or zero. Without a distinct adequate answerability/correctness reference, strict supported-answer yield and conditional legal-error rates remain unavailable. Historical system refusal flags likewise do not independently establish semantic answer status.

\subsection{Source checks and sampling limits}
The source-check frame was frozen using 40 distinct matched queries,10 per category, selected once without replacement from sorted IDs with seed 42; both arms yield 80 outcomes. Inclusion probabilities are 1/10 for A/B and 2/15 for C/D. This is a post-hoc audit of already exposed data, not a new blinded human study or preregistration. The probability component is kept separate from targeted historical fatal cases, positive-category refusal flags and external adverse records. No unweighted population error rate is calculated from the combined set.

The audit checks actual verifier-message document identities, titles, dates and normalized Holding/Snippet spans against preserved CAP casebody material. Normalization covers Unicode typography and whitespace, not paraphrase equivalence. Locating a span in a reporter headnote, majority opinion or other opinion is a source-location fact, not certification that it is the Court's holding. Unlocated text is not automatically fabricated: cleaning, truncation and unavailable sources remain possible. Candidate quotation-shaped strings are flagged for location checks without assuming they quote a court. Source-date checks against explicit as-of-year phrases do not prove answer leakage or controlling-law applicability.

Across the 80 sampled arm inputs, 948 document occurrences were parsed; primary CAP files were available for 948 occurrences. There were 0 title mismatches and 0 date mismatches under the stated exact metadata comparison. Of 1896 normalized Holding/Snippet span checks, 1873 were located and 23 were not located. These occurrence counts are descriptive provenance checks, not independent case counts or legal-accuracy estimates. The separate targeted ledger accounts for 20 historical records and 669 external adverse-flag records, with unavailable identity/interpretation recorded rather than guessed.

These mechanical source checks do not adjudicate complete answer correctness, disputed doctrine or the sufficiency of the evidence for each legal task. Existing assessor decisions are preserved; no new human ratings are attributed to the authors. Unresolved source identity and legal interpretation remain explicit unknowns. The prior workbook Column N/O correction task is not silently reopened.

\subsection{Verifier error-rate feasibility}
All 700 matched verifier inputs survive as exact HTTP request bodies. Their raw UTF-8 SHA-256 values match both the recorded request event and accounting reservation; query text matches the frozen benchmark. The request bodies, rather than the verifier's own reason or later reconstructed graph text, define the audited inputs. There is one final verifier decision per matched arm; unlogged historical retry states cannot be recovered from final contexts.

An independently supported reference on actual query/context sufficiency is still absent. Source-span presence, benchmark answerability, the verifier's explanation and a judge's high score for abstention are not that reference. For example, B001's T response receives faithfulness 1.0 for abstaining; that judgment alone establishes neither justified refusal nor false rejection. Consequently, verifier false-acceptance and false-rejection rates are not estimated. If a valid reference becomes available, false rejection divides rejected sufficient contexts by all reference-sufficient contexts; false acceptance divides passed insufficient contexts by all reference-insufficient contexts. Unsupported among accepted answers is a different conditional outcome. No confusion matrix, legal-expert validation or counterfactual answer for a blocked generator is fabricated.

The existing author-rating proxies remain unchanged. In particular, Author 2's six uncertain non-refusals remain separate: contradicted-among-non-refusals is 12/65, with a descriptive unresolved-label envelope 12/65 to 18/65; this is not a population confidence interval. Original/reconciled source ratings, automated faithfulness, response-type proxies and factual source-span checks answer different questions and are not pooled. The expert-assessment part of the reviewer request remains unfulfilled; this narrower audit documents observable outcomes and unavailable validation rather than claiming legal reliability.
