## Limits and risks to accept explicitly before a run

- 300 seconds is the supervised child lifetime, including child setup and dependency verification—not total parent time. Authentic timing margin is unknown.
- 4 GiB limits the child Windows Job tree, not parent or machine memory. Authentic peak use and Windows behavior remain untested.
- 16 MiB bounds child output capture, not total evidence/copy disk consumption. Earlier free-space readings do not reserve space.
- Boundary PowerShell calls have separate source-default 30-second and 1-MiB bounds per call. Rule-property read-back is not proof of network traffic enforcement.
- The verified controller may coexist with the child, but is not exempt from the interpreter-wide network block. Other known matching processes stop rule creation; unreadable-process uncertainty remains.
- Cleanup failure is failure. If child-tree termination cannot be confirmed, the reviewed implementation retains the rule and records recovery instructions rather than deleting a protection while descendants may remain alive. Cleanup is not guaranteed. No retry is permitted after an authorized attempt.
- A firewall rule scoped to the interpreter can affect other software using that interpreter. Concurrency checks cannot prove absence of unreadable processes.
